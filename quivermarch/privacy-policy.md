Quiver March – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Quiver March is a game by lindmarkscreek. This policy explains what information the game handles.

No accounts, no ads, no purchases

The game has no sign-in, no advertising and no in-app purchases. It does not ask for your name, email,
contacts, photos, location or any other personal information, and it does not use the advertising ID.

Stored on your device

Your level progress and settings (music, sound, volumes, the "Share stats" switch) are saved on your device
only. They are removed when you uninstall the game.

Anonymous gameplay statistics

To learn which levels are too hard or too easy and to find crashes, the game sends anonymous gameplay
statistics to GameAnalytics, which processes them for us as a service provider.

What is sent:





a random install ID created by the game (not a device ID, not the advertising ID, and not linked to you);



level starts, wins and losses, retries, where in a level soldiers were lost, upgrades chosen;



session length and number, the game version, frame rate;



the phone model, operating system version and error messages if the game hits an error.

As with any internet connection, your IP address is seen by GameAnalytics when the data is sent; GameAnalytics
uses it to estimate the country.

The data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and is not sold or shared with anyone else. See
GameAnalytics' privacy policy for how they handle it.

You can turn this off at any time: Settings → SHARE STATS: OFF. The game then sends nothing.

Deleting your data

The statistics are anonymous: they are not linked to your name or account, so we cannot look them up by
who you are. Turning off Share stats stops all further sending, and uninstalling the game deletes the
install ID. For any question or deletion request, email us.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

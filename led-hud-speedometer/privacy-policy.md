# Privacy Policy – GPS Speedometer: Car HUD

_Last updated: 22 October 2026_

GPS Speedometer: Car HUD ("the app") is a GPS speedometer for Android, published by lindmarkscreek
("we"). This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

## Summary

- Your **location never leaves your phone**. It is used only while the app is open, to measure
  speed, and is never stored or transmitted.
- The app **collects no data**. It has no analytics, no account and no network access.
- There are **no ads**, no advertising ID, and no sale of data.

## Location

The app asks for precise location ("while using the app" only) to read your speed from GPS.
Location is processed on your device in real time. It is not saved, not kept as a history,
and not sent to us or to anyone else. The app never requests background location.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores your settings (speed unit, display colour, HUD brightness and whether you have
seen the safety notice) on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

## Children

The app is intended for drivers and is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

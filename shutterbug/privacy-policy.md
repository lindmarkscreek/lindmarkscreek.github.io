# Privacy Policy – Shutterbug: Photo Editor

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

Shutterbug: Photo Editor ("the app") is a photo editor for Android, published by lindmarkscreek
("we"). This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

## Summary

- Your **photos never leave your phone** unless you choose to share them. They are edited on
  your device.
- The app **collects no data**. It has no analytics, no account and no network access.
- There are **no ads**, no advertising ID, and no sale of data.

## Photos

The app only opens a photo that you pick in it or send to it from another app. It reads that
photo on your device to show and edit it. Your original photo is never changed.

When you tap **Save**, the edited copy is saved to your device's photo library
(Pictures/Shutterbug). When you tap **Share**, the app makes a temporary copy on your device and
hands it to the app you choose in Android's share menu. What that app does with it is covered by
its own privacy policy.

## Data stored on your device

The app remembers small preferences on your device, such as your preferred strength for each
filter. They are not backed up or transferred. Uninstalling the app removes them. Photos you
saved stay in your photo library until you delete them.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
